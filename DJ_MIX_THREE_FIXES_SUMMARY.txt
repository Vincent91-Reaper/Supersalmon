DJ MIX THREE FIXES SUMMARY
==========================

Date: 2026-02-11
Commit: 97876af

PROBLEMS REPORTED
-----------------

1. Artists listed multiple times
   Example: "Shades", "Shades & ID" instead of "Shades", "ID"

2. Title includes "(DJ Mix)" suffix
   Example: "Shades - SHADES at DEF: Underground (DJ Mix)" 
   Expected: "Shades - SHADES at DEF: Underground"

3. BB code formatting broken
   Example: [artist]Alix Perez, Shades & Eprom[/artist]
   Expected: [artist]Alix Perez[/artist], [artist]Shades[/artist], [artist]Eprom[/artist]

SOLUTIONS IMPLEMENTED
--------------------

Fix 1: Artist Splitting
- Split artist strings on " & " separator
- Applied in both scraped and file-based metadata extraction
- File: brucelee94/uploader/__init__.py (2 locations)
- Result: "Shades & ID" becomes ["Shades", "ID"]

Fix 2: Title Cleanup
- Remove "(DJ Mix)" suffix from title after detection
- Applied in both scraped and file-based metadata processing
- File: brucelee94/uploader/__init__.py (2 locations)
- Result: Clean group name without suffix

Fix 3: BB Code Formatting
- Split track artists on " & " before creating [artist] tags
- Applied in both multi-disc and single-disc description generation
- File: brucelee94/uploader/upload.py (2 locations)
- Result: Each artist in separate [artist] tags
