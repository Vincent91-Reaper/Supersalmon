                   TRACK ARTIST FORMAT - EXAMPLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BEFORE (Old Format)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
All artists mixed together before the title:

[b]Various Artists - Album Title[/b]
January 15, 2024

[b]01.[/b] [artist]Jon Hansen[/artist], [artist]Mary Doufle[/artist] - My love is forever [i](03:45)[/i]
[b]02.[/b] [artist]Artist A[/artist], [artist]Guest B[/artist] - Another Track [i](04:20)[/i]
[b]03.[/b] [artist]Main Act[/artist], [artist]Feature[/artist] - Song Title [i](03:30)[/i]

❌ Problem: Cannot distinguish main artists from featured/guest artists


AFTER (New Format)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Main artists before title, featured artists after in (feat. ...):

[b]Various Artists - Album Title[/b]
January 15, 2024

[b]01.[/b] [artist]Jon Hansen[/artist] - My love is forever (feat. [artist]Mary Doufle[/artist]) [i](03:45)[/i]
[b]02.[/b] [artist]Artist A[/artist] - Another Track (feat. [artist]Guest B[/artist]) [i](04:20)[/i]
[b]03.[/b] [artist]Main Act[/artist] - Song Title (feat. [artist]Feature[/artist]) [i](03:30)[/i]

✓ Solution: Clear separation between main and featured artists

KEY POINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✓ Main artists appear before " - " separator
✓ Track title in the middle
✓ Guest/featured artists after title in "(feat. ...)"
✓ Multiple artists of same type separated by ", "
✓ Each artist wrapped in [artist]...[/artist] tags
✓ Duration at the end in italics: [i](MM:SS)[/i]


APPLICABILITY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Applied to:   Various Artists albums (3+ main artists)
Not applied:  Regular albums (1-2 main artists)
              → Regular albums show album artist in header only

Multi-disc:   Format works identically for multi-disc albums
              Each disc has same track format


BENEFITS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✓ Clear distinction between artist roles
✓ Follows music industry standard convention
✓ Better readability and user experience
✓ Compatible with RED BBCode artist linking
✓ Consistent format across all tracks
